## Key Principle: NO Quantitative Material Weakness Threshold

**Material Weakness Definition:**
"A deficiency, or combination of deficiencies, in internal control over financial reporting such that there is a **reasonable possibility** that [the auditor] will not prevent, or detect and correct, a material misstatement of the annual or interim financial statements." — PCAOB AS 2201, Appendix A

**Critical Point:** There is **NO bright-line percentage, dollar amount, or quantitative test** for materiality. The "reasonable possibility" standard requires auditor professional judgment based on the specific facts and circumstances.

## Bundle Structure

**Bundle Pattern:** Thornigal Orthogonal Regions (Event-Sourced)

**Regions:**

1. **control_lifecycle** — Tracks the ICFR control design, testing, evaluation, and remediation cycle
   - Initial: `FISCAL_YEAR_START`
   - Terminal: `OPINION_FILED`, `DEFICIENCY_DISCLOSED`
   - Events: 15 transitions covering the full control assessment lifecycle

2. **filer_compliance** — Tracks filer category classification, timeline determination, and filing deadline compliance
   - Initial: `FILER_CATEGORY_UNKNOWN`
   - Terminal: `FILING_DEADLINE_MET`, `FILING_DEADLINE_MISSED`
   - Events: 10 transitions mapping filer type to filing deadline

**Design Principle:** Event-driven transitions only. No quantitative guard conditions. All regulatory thresholds (filer size, filing deadlines, penalty amounts) are metadata only and can be updated independently without breaking state machine reachability.
